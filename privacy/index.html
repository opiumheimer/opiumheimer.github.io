Potion Pop Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Potion Pop is a game made by ovdruss (Russel). This policy explains what information the game and its ad partner handle.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your settings (sound, music, vibration, colorblind), your best score, your Undo and Shuffle counts, the free-refill timer, your unfinished run, your Story progress (levels won, stars, coins, hearts, power-ups, shop repairs and the story scenes you've seen) and your Daily Challenge best and streak. Uninstalling the game deletes them from the device.
  • The game plays fully offline. When your phone is online, it also does the things below.

Your player account and cloud save

  • A guest account. The first time the game is online, it creates an anonymous player account for you on Supabase (a database service; the data is stored in Singapore). It is a random ID with a random player name made by the game (like "Fizzy Otter 42"): no real name, email or phone number.
  • What is saved there: your settings are not; your game progress is: your best score, your Undo and Shuffle counts, your Story progress (coins, hearts, power-ups, stars, repairs, scenes seen), your Daily Challenge best and streak, and a log of the game actions that change them (for example "bought a Hammer", "won level 7 with 3 stars", "watched an ad for a heart"). The game uses the log to keep your save the same on the server and to stop cheating. It is used for nothing else and is never sold or shared.
  • Sign in with Google (optional). If you tap "Sign in with Google" in Settings, Google tells the game your name, your email address and your profile picture, and your save is linked to your Google account so you can get it back on another phone. Supabase stores your name and email with your account. They are never shown to other players: you keep your random player name. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • The leaderboard. Your weekly Endless score and your Daily Challenge score are shown to other players with your random player name.
  • Game updates. The game downloads game data (balance numbers, the Daily board, level fixes) from the same service, and asks GitHub whether a newer version of the game exists. These requests carry no information about you (like any internet request, the server sees your IP address).
  • The time check. To count free refills fairly, the game asks Google's server for the current time (a plain request to google.com that carries no information about you; Google sees your IP address).
  • Deleting your data. Email the address below with your player name (Settings shows it, under "Sign in with Google") and I will delete your account, your save and your scores. Guest accounts that are never used again may be deleted after a year.
  • The game has no analytics of its own.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The game shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one, for example for an extra Undo or Shuffle. The ads come from Google AdMob.
  • To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses it to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, where the law and your choices allow, to personalize ads.
  • How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads use your information. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings, under Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads, or Settings, then Privacy, then Ads, depending on the phone).
  • Ads need an internet connection. The game itself plays offline.

Children

Potion Pop is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.
