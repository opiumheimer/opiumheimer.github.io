The shop needs you.

Aunt Elara left Mika the keys to her potion shop, and not much else. The lamps are dead, the windows are boarded up, and nobody will say why.

Win levels, earn stars, and fix the place up one repair at a time.

Wait, a sorting game with a story?

  1. Play a level30 in Chapter 1, each one made by hand.
  2. Earn starsUp to three a level. Fewer moves, more stars.
  3. Fix the shopSpend stars on repairs: the counter, the windows, the shelves…
  4. Walk the mapEach repair opens the next stretch of Potion Quarter.
  5. Hear what happenedThe story plays out as you go. No spoilers here.

You get 5 hearts. Lose a level, lose a heart; they refill while you're away.

Three kinds of puzzle

Story levels mix three puzzles. A solver checks every level, so every one can be won with three stars.

  • Sort

    Pour the top drink onto its match. Hit the goal before your moves run out.

  • Cabinet

    Three bottles of the same shape pop. The back row slides forward.

  • Deliveries

    Tap a bottle and it slides till it hits something. Clear the counter.

Endless: chase your best

No levels, no finish line. New drinks keep dripping in, the shelf keeps growing, and every clear feeds your combo.

  • Combos
  • Sugar Rush: double points
  • Undo & Shuffle
  • More bottles as you score

Daily Challenge

One board, the same for everyone, new every day. Keep your streak going.

Installing the APK
  1. Tap Download on your Android phone to get PotionPop.apk.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then come back.
  4. Follow Android's install screen and tap Install.
  5. Already playing? Install the new version over the old one: your progress stays.

About the Play Protect message

Because Potion Pop is distributed outside Google Play for now, Android or Play Protect may show an extra warning or ask you to confirm the install. Only download the APK from this page.

What the app asks Android for

  • Internet and network state: cloud save, leaderboards, the Daily board and ads.
  • Vibration: a little buzz when bottles pop (you can turn it off).
  • The advertising ID, basic phone state, wake lock and a foreground service, added by Google's ads and services libraries.

None of these are the permissions Android asks you about: no contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone. Details in the privacy policy.
