🛡️ Android says "App blocked" or "Unsafe app"?

That's normal for any game that isn't on the Play Store yet: Google just hasn't seen it before. It's safe: no virus, and it never touches your photos, messages or contacts.

Tap More details, then Install anyway. (If it offers Scan app, tap that first: it checks the game and lets it through.)

What's inside

  • Endless: no levels, just chase your best score
  • Cute bottles with faces that cheer, worry and pout
  • 8 sweet drinks, from Strawberry Milk to Cola
  • Chain clears into combos and a SUGAR RUSH for double points
  • The shelf grows as you score: more bottles, more drinks
  • Undo and Shuffle, with free refills every 30 minutes
  • Colorblind mode, one thumb, plays offline

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded PotionPop.apk.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  4. If a Play Protect box says the app is blocked or unsafe, tap More details → Install anyway. It only means the game isn't on the Play Store yet.
  5. Got an older version? Install the new one over it: your progress stays.
