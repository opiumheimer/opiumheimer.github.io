Story Mode · Chapter 1

Aunt Elara left Mika a potion shop. And a mystery.

The lamps are dead, the windows are boarded up and the cat is suspiciously well fed. Win levels, earn stars and fix the place up one repair at a time, while the Potion Quarter tells you what really happened here.

  1. Win levels Up to 3 stars each
  2. Fix the shop 12 repairs, from the counter to the cellar
  3. Hear the story 23 painted scenes, no spoilers here

Meet the neighbours

Juno, the second-fastest delivery girl in Potion Quarter. Sable, who answers a different question than the one you asked. Mocha the calico, who sits on the important thing. Everyone knows a little more than they say.

Three puzzles, one story

Pour it. Match it. Slide it.

Story levels switch between three kinds of puzzle. A solver checks every level, so every one can be won with three stars.

  • Sort

    Pour the top drink onto its match. Fill a bottle with one drink and it pops.

  • Cabinet

    Three bottles of the same shape on a shelf pop. The ones behind slide forward.

  • Deliveries

    Tap a bottle and it slides till it hits something. Fill Juno's orders before her tray overflows.

5 hearts that refill while you're away, and five powers to unlock along the way: Undo, Peek, Shuffle, Rescue and the Hammer.

Keep it lit

Win in a row, start with extra moves.

Beat new levels on the first try, one after another, and your streak grows. At 3 wins in a row your next level starts with +1 move, at 5 with +2, at 7 with +3. Lose one and the flame goes out.

How do I install the APK?
  1. Tap Download on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then come back.
  3. Tap Install. Already playing? Install over your old version, without uninstalling: your progress stays.
  4. That's the last install: from now on the game updates itself.

Want to check the file? Its SHA-256 is 4f045ce98a7f58d8e09786636cf49c0f4b91e6653a3160050d16f30008121cdf

Android or Play Protect shows a warning

Potion Pop isn't on Google Play yet, so Android may ask you to confirm an install from outside the store. Only download the APK from this page.

Do I need to be online?

No, it plays offline. Online you get updates, the Daily board, leaderboards and cloud save (sign in with Google in Settings to take your progress to a new phone).

What does the app ask Android for?
  • Internet and network state: updates, cloud save, leaderboards, the Daily board and ads.
  • Notifications: the heart reminders (Android 13 and newer asks you first), and starting after a reboot so they still arrive.
  • Vibration: a little buzz when bottles pop (you can turn it off).
  • The advertising ID and a few more, added by Google's ads libraries.

No contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone. Details in the privacy policy.
